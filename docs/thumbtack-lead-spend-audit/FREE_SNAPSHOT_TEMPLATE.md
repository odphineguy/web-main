## Required footer

This snapshot describes observed performance in the supplied period. It is not a forecast and does not guarantee more leads, hires, revenue, or a lower cost per hire. Small samples and incomplete exports can materially change the result.

### Sample-size labels

- **Large:** 100+ leads in the reported segment.
- **Moderate:** 30–99 leads.
- **Small:** 10–29 leads.
- **Insufficient:** fewer than 10 leads, zero matched hires, or missing fields required for the calculation.
